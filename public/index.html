Project Data Sources & Operational Benchmark Architecture

EXTERNAL DATASETS & RECONFIGURABLE SOP DATASET: Grounded in open surveillance benchmarks and deterministic rule configuration datasets.
1. VisDrone Dataset GitHub Repository ↗

High-angle pole and drone surveillance video dataset. Essential benchmark for high watchtower CCTV viewpoints where individuals and vehicles appear at small pixel scales (20–40px) at steep oblique angles.

2. BDD100K Dataset GitHub Repository ↗

Diverse driving and road surveillance dataset covering adverse environmental conditions: heavy rain, dense fog, night glare, and dusk/dawn transitions representative of Himalayan border patrol tracks.

3. UFPR-ALPR Dataset Database Portal ↗

Real-world multi-frame vehicle video tracks specifically recorded at barrier gates and inspection choke points. Validates multi-frame temporal OCR consistency where single-frame OCR produces false readings.

4. OpenStreetMap (OSM) Border GIS OpenStreetMap ↗

Supplies open geospatial coordinates for Border Outposts (BOPs), transit check posts, access roads, and international boundary zero-line coordinates for our tactical C2 GIS display.

IBVAP Red-Team Architecture & Threat Mitigations

Intelligent Border Video Analytics Platform (SIH 2026, PS 26187 - SSB)
This operational platform was built strictly following the findings of IBVAP_RedTeam_Review.md to solve real-world border challenges.

1. Challenging Uniform CCTV Assumptions (Section 3 & 11)

Unlike naive proposals that claim "ANPR and Face Recognition everywhere", this architecture recognizes wide-angle perimeter CCTV is optically incapable of reliable face/plate recognition. ANPR and Face detection are implemented as strictly conditional capabilities active only at choke points (e.g. CAM-02 Barrier Gate) with multi-frame temporal confirmation.

2. Anti-Flooding Alert Engine (Section 15)

Prevents operator alarm fatigue. A continuous zone loiter produces ONE live event with an in-place duration counter (e.g. LIVE: 14s), not 60 separate alerts per second. Cooldown periods suppress borderline oscillation triggers.

3. Edge-First Hybrid AI & WAN Loss Resilience (Section 6 & 8)

Remote BOPs experience WAN interruptions. All detection, tracking, virtual fence checks, and local alarms run locally on the edge node. When WAN disconnects (testable via the "Simulate WAN Loss" button), events buffer locally. Upon reconnection, an idempotent batch sync occurs with zero loss.

4. Deterministic Explainable Rules & Chain of Custody (Section 13 & 16)

Replaced opaque "behavior AI" with auditable rules: polygon virtual fence, dwell timers, directional tripwires, and curfew enforcement. Every evidence record is stamped with a cryptographic SHA-256 checksum to ensure legal chain of custody.
